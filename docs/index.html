The engine and the window.

OpenSpread

The engine, on your computer.

  • Reads public prices from Binance, OKX, Bybit, Kraken and Coinbase.
  • Shows the net spread after both taker fees. Negative stays negative.
  • Your exchanges and pairs in one local file, ~/.openspread/config.json.
  • Spread alerts that ring once and re-arm, so they do not flicker.
  • NEWBuilt-in paper trading, with a risk gate before every simulated order.
  • NEWMCP for your Claude or Codex: read data, record analyses and test simulated robots. Real orders do not exist there.
  • Serves it on 127.0.0.1, with no server of ours: the only requests out are public ones to exchanges, chains and the feeds you pick.
OpenSpread on GitHub

Bullroom

The window: your bots as an office.

  • Seated means in a position; walking around means flat.
  • PnL floats over each head; the room sign sums the room.
  • Markets panel: choose exchanges and pairs, and set alerts, from the room.
  • A bot that hits its daily loss limit is ejected from the room.
  • NEWSimulation room and Robots panel: start, stop and see why each robot stopped.
  • NEWAnalyses panel: hit rate only with a sample, calibration, and results already after fees.
  • Simulated in the browser, or live with OpenSpread.
▶ Try the demo Bullroom on GitHub

Two repositories. Node 22.12 or newer.

1. OpenSpread (live prices)

git clone https://github.com/JVMarks/OpenSpread.git
cd OpenSpread/core
npm install
npm start
# OpenSpread core on 127.0.0.1:4848 (read-only)

2. Bullroom (the window)

git clone https://github.com/JVMarks/bullroom.git
cd bullroom
npm install
npm run dev:client
# open http://localhost:5173/?source=core

Choose exchanges, pairs and alerts in Bullroom (click the LIVE badge, or Settings › Markets), or edit ~/.openspread/config.json. Simulated robots: Settings › Robots. Only Bullroom? Open ?mock=trading for simulated bots, no exchange needed.

What does not change.

  • Local-first

    No server of ours, no cookies, no tracking. It runs on your computer.

  • Your keys stay home

    When keys arrive, they stay on your machine, never with withdrawal permission.

  • The AI proposes

    The engine executes, inside a risk policy you set. The AI never sends an order.

  • Honest numbers

    Losses are shown. Simulated is always labeled. Negative stays negative.

Built in stages, in the open.

  1. DONEBullroom: simulated bots, PnL over heads, room signs, bot card with 5m/1h/24h.
  2. DONEOpenSpread core: live, read-only net spreads from five exchanges.
  3. DONEConfigurable exchanges and pairs, with the Markets panel in Bullroom.
  4. DONESpread alerts that ring once and re-arm.
  5. DONEEconomic calendar and radar in the room; machine room (API limits, latency).
  6. DONEBuilt-in paper trading: robots simulated on the real order book, with a risk gate and their own room. No keys, no real orders.
  7. DONEMCP: your own Claude or Codex reads the data, records analyses and tests simulated robots, with a methodically scored track record. No tool can place a real order.
  8. NEXTReal orders with a risk policy and approval per order, using Hummingbot.
  9. LATERNostr: identity, signed analyses, threads and zaps.
